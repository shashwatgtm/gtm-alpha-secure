What to do next

  • Founder: Run the free EPIC audit first to score your go to market motions before committing budget or hiring.
  • CEO: Use the free audit report with your leadership team, and book a conversation if you need hands-on fractional CMO support.
  • CMO: Run the free EPIC audit and MCP tools to build your 180 day plan, then reach out if you want a working session on your results.
  • CRO: Score your current sales motion with the free audit and use the output to align territories and pipeline targets.
  • Demand generation head: Use the free audit and roadmap tools in your browser or assistant to plan your next 90 days of execution.

Why come back

Re-run the free EPIC audit whenever your contract value, deal cycle, or stage changes, or come back here when your team is ready for a custom consulting engagement. The tool applies the same fixed rules every time. It does not call an AI model, and this site does not keep what you enter.

Choose your app

Run the free audit in your browser. Copy its result into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini when you want help acting on it. The MCP connector works with supported plans in Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, VS Code and Windsurf. Gemini CLI and Antigravity use the separate Helix GTM Suite skills package.
