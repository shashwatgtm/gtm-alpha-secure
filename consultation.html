How do you know which GTM motion fits your B2B SaaS deal size and sales cycle?

Score your business across the four EPIC motions (Ecosystem and ABM, Product-Led, Inbound and Outbound, and Community) from 1 to 10 using your annual contract value, sales cycle length, net revenue retention, and addressable account count. The diagnostic names the highest-scoring motion to lead with and maps a Days 1 to 180 plan in the free EPIC audit.

The form sends its answers as a standard form post to the address in its action. Where a list sends a code that differs from the words you see, these are the codes:

  • team_size: 1 (Solo Founder), 5 (2 to 5 people), 10 (6 to 10 people), 20 (11 to 20 people), 50 (21 to 50 people), 100 (51 to 100 people), 200 (More than 100 people)
  • monthly_budget: 500 (Under $500), 1000 ($500 to $1,000), 5000 ($1,000 to $5,000), 10000 ($5,000 to $10,000), 25000 ($10,000 to $25,000), 50000 ($25,000 to $50,000), 100000 (Over $50,000)

How to read the answer

Each consultation scores the four EPIC go to market motions (Ecosystem and ABM, Product-Led, Inbound and Outbound, and Community) from 1 to 10 based on your stage, industry, deal size, sales cycle, NRR, TAM, self-serve capability, deal source, and geography. You get a recommended lead motion and a secondary motion, any warnings, and a Days 1 to 180 plan in four stages (Days 1 to 30, Days 31 to 60, Days 61 to 90, and Days 91 to 180).

What to do next

  • Founder: Compare the lead EPIC motion against the runner-up on the same buyer problem and record what buyer observation would change the choice.
  • CEO: Ask finance to verify your supplied annual contract value and retention figures before committing budget, and agree a spending limit and stop condition for the first motion test.
  • CMO: Separate your supplied channel description from measured buyer acceptance before treating current channel activity as proof of motion fit.
  • CRO: Check your entered deal cycle and primary deal source against closed-won and closed-lost deals before changing sales coverage or quotas.
  • Demand generation head: Run the Days 1 to 30 steps on a dated sample of your addressable accounts and measure reach and qualified conversations before scaling into Days 31 to 180.

Why come back

Re-run the consultation when your average contract value shifts, when you move upmarket to a new buyer segment, or when your stage or geography changes. The tool applies the same fixed rules every time. It does not call an AI model, and this site keeps only the GTM challenge from the audit form, for 90 days.
