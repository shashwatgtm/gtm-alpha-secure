For developers

The form posts its answers as a normal form to the address in its action. For the lists whose codes differ from the words you see, it sends these codes:

  • team_size: 1 (Solo Founder), 5 (2 to 5 people), 10 (6 to 10 people), 20 (11 to 20 people), 50 (21 to 50 people), 100 (51 to 100 people), 200 (100+ people)
  • monthly_budget: 500 (Under $500), 1000 ($500 to $1,000), 5000 ($1,000 to $5,000), 10000 ($5,000 to $10,000), 25000 ($10,000 to $25,000), 50000 ($25,000 to $50,000), 100000 (Over $50,000)
  • acv_band: under_5k (Under $5,000), 5k_to_50k ($5,000 to $50,000), over_50k (Over $50,000)
  • deal_cycle_band: under_14_days (Under 14 days), 14_to_90_days (14 to 90 days), over_90_days (Over 90 days)
  • nrr_band: under_100 (Under 100%), 100_to_120 (100% to 120%), over_120 (Over 120%)
  • tam_band: under_500 (Under 500), 500_to_10000 (500 to 10,000), over_10000 (Over 10,000)
  • deal_source: referrals (Referrals), outbound (Outbound), partnerships (Partnerships), inbound (Inbound), mixed (Mixed, no single source)
  • geography: india (India), us_eu (US or Europe), middle_east (Middle East), apac (APAC outside India), global (Global or several regions)
  • self_serve: yes (Yes), no (No)
