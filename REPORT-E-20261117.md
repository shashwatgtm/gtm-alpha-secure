## Buyer situation and before

A buyer requested a 120 day inbound and outbound plan. The connected roadmap tool returned a schedule ending on day 90 and labeled the timeframe “default, not supplied”. The canonical `timeframe: "120-day"` produced this result with a final note that the value had been left out. A second call using `horizon_days: 120` produced the same default. Both scoring tools also returned scores after receiving `South America` as geography or `trade fairs` as deal source. All four calls silently lost a scoring input until a note at the end of the answer.

## Why it matters to a buyer

A team could set its launch dates from the wrong plan or choose a motion using a score that omitted its stated market or sales channel.

## After

The roadmap accepts supported 30, 60 and 90 day durations, including existing synonyms, and checks the supplied day count for conflicts. An unsupported or conflicting horizon prompts the buyer to choose one of the three supported durations. An omitted horizon retains the labeled default. Both scoring tools ask for a supported geography or deal source before scoring. Recognized synonyms retain their existing meaning.
