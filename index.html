The EPIC framework

The four motions GTM Alpha scores

EPIC is Shashwat Ghosh's go-to-market framework. GTM Alpha scores each motion for your company and names the one to lead with.

E

Ecosystem and ABM

Win named accounts through account-based marketing and partners.

P

Product-Led Growth

Let the product win and grow customers, with sales where it helps.

I

Inbound and Outbound

Create demand with content and direct outreach.

C

Community-Led

Grow through a community of customers and peers who speak for you.

Questions and answers

How is the EPIC score calculated?

Each motion starts from a default for the company's stage (Seed, Series A, Series B, Series C or later, Bootstrapped). Fixed adjustments follow for contract value, deal cycle, net revenue retention, number of addressable accounts, self-serve, main deal source and region. Product-Led is capped at 4 in logistics, procurement, manufacturing, healthcare and government. Scores are kept between 1 and 10, then three checks can add a warning: a leaky bucket, AI search disruption of inbound, and a hybrid motion. The company name never affects the score.

How do I use GTM Alpha in Claude?

In Claude, open Customize, then Connectors, select +, then Add custom connector, and paste https://gtmalpha.gtmhelix.com/mcp with no sign-in. Then ask, for example: Use GTM Alpha to run an EPIC audit on our GTM. The Use it in Claude or ChatGPT page has the steps for ChatGPT and other AI assistants.

What data does GTM Alpha keep?

The free EPIC audit form and GTM Alpha in Claude store and log nothing: your answers build the result in that request and are then dropped. The EPIC audit API for developers does not keep new audits; older records are deleted on request within 30 days. The privacy policy lists every field.

Who is GTM Alpha not ideal for?

Teams that want an AI-written strategy: the EPIC scores follow fixed rules from the documented rubric, and no AI model writes them. Companies that cannot share basic numbers: without contract value, deal cycle, net revenue retention and target account count, the result is marked preliminary. Anyone who needs live market data: GTM Alpha looks nothing up and works only from what you enter.
