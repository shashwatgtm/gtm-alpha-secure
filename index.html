Which GTM motion should you lead with?

GTM Alpha is a free tool for founders, CMOs and CROs, built by Shashwat Ghosh of Helix GTM Consulting. It scores the four EPIC go to market motions (Ecosystem and ABM, Product-Led, Inbound and Outbound, Community-Led) from 1 to 10 using fixed rules, tells you which motion to lead with and suggests a plan. To use it in an AI app, add https://gtmalpha.gtmhelix.com/mcp in any supported MCP client; you do not need to sign in.

Questions and answers

How is the EPIC score calculated?

Each motion starts from a default for the company's stage (Seed, Series A, Series B, Series C or later, Bootstrapped). Fixed adjustments are then made for contract value, deal cycle, net revenue retention, number of addressable accounts, self serve, main deal source and region. Product-Led is capped at 4 in logistics, procurement, manufacturing, healthcare and government. Scores are kept between 1 and 10, and three checks can then add a warning: a leaky bucket, AI search disrupting inbound, and a hybrid motion. The company name never affects the score.

How do I use GTM Alpha in Claude?

In Claude, open Customize, then Connectors, select + and choose Add custom connector, then paste https://gtmalpha.gtmhelix.com/mcp. No sign in is needed. Then ask, for example: Use GTM Alpha to run an EPIC audit on our GTM. The Use it in Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini page has the steps for ChatGPT and other apps.

What data does GTM Alpha keep?

GTM Alpha in Claude does not store or log any of your inputs or results. The free EPIC audit form keeps only your GTM challenge, with your company domain, for 90 days, and only when you give a company website; your other answers build the result for that request and are then dropped. The EPIC audit API for developers does not keep new audits, and older records are deleted on request within 30 days. The privacy policy lists every field.

Who is GTM Alpha not ideal for?

It is not ideal for teams that want a strategy written by AI, because the EPIC scores follow fixed rules from the documented rubric and no AI model writes them. It is not ideal for companies that cannot share basic numbers: if contract value, deal cycle, net revenue retention or target account count is missing, the result is marked preliminary. It is also not ideal for anyone who needs live market data, because GTM Alpha does not look anything up and works only from what you enter.

When a tool is not enough: if the decision needs more than one answer, talk it through in a call. Book 45 minutes with Shashwat.

How to read the answer

Each go to market motion receives a score from 1 to 10 based on your stage, contract value, deal cycle, net revenue retention, and addressable accounts, along with the primary and secondary motion and any warnings. When optional figures are left out, the result is marked preliminary so you know which inputs to gather before treating the score as final.

What to do next

  • Founder: Pick the primary motion and cut spend on motions that scored poorly for your contract value and deal cycle.
  • CEO: Use the EPIC motion scores and rule adjustments to align the board and leadership team around one primary motion and one secondary motion.
  • CMO: Shift marketing budget and content toward the primary motion and address any warnings flagged by the audit.
  • CRO: Match sales hiring, territory design, and pipeline targets to the lead motion and the 30 to 90 day roadmap.
  • Demand generation head: Run the first 30 days of channel actions for the lead motion and track conversion against your deal cycle.

Why come back

Re-run GTM Alpha when your average contract value changes, when you enter a new region or segment, or when you move to the next funding stage. The tool applies the same fixed rules every time. It does not call an AI model, and this site keeps only the GTM challenge from the audit form, for 90 days.

Choose your app

Run the free audit in your browser. Copy its result into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini when you want help acting on it. The MCP connector works with supported plans in Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, VS Code and Windsurf. Gemini CLI and Antigravity use the separate Helix GTM Suite skills package.

Use it in Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini · How to install

Use GTM Alpha in ChatGPT

  1. Open ChatGPT Plugins and choose the option to add a custom MCP server. Your account and workspace must allow it.
  2. Name the connection GTM Alpha, enter https://gtmalpha.gtmhelix.com/mcp and choose no authentication. Review the connection notice.
  3. Create the connection, review its tools and install it. Select it with an @ mention in a chat, ask for an EPIC audit and supply your stage, industry and GTM facts.
