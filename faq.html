🔐 Data & Privacy

What data does GTM Alpha collect?

We collect only what's necessary:

  • Contact Info: Your name (the form does not ask for email)
  • Business Info: Company name, description, website, LinkedIn URL, challenge, stage, team size and budget (for consultations)
  • Usage Data: None collected by our pages; Netlify, our host, keeps standard request logs
  • MCP: Nothing is stored or logged by our code

We do NOT collect: passwords, financial details (handled by PayPal), or tracking cookies.

Is my business data kept confidential?

Absolutely. All business information shared during consultations is treated as strictly confidential. We:

  • Never share your data with competitors
  • Don't use your data for training AI models
  • Only share with the service providers needed to run the site (Netlify for hosting, PayPal for payments)
How can I access, correct, or delete my data?

You have full control over your data. Email shashwat@hyperplays.in with:

  • "Data Access Request" - to get a copy of your data
  • "Data Correction Request" - to fix inaccurate information
  • "Data Deletion Request" - to have your data erased

We respond within 30 days as required by GDPR, DPDP (India), and CCPA.

Are you GDPR and DPDP compliant?

Yes. We comply with:

  • GDPR (EU General Data Protection Regulation)
  • DPDP Act 2023 (India's Digital Personal Data Protection)
  • CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act)

See our Privacy Policy for complete details.

Do you use cookies or tracking?

No. Our pages set no cookies and include no analytics or tracking scripts:

  • No third-party trackers: No Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or ad tracking
  • Fonts: Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address
  • PayPal: Sets its own cookies on its checkout pages

🛠️ MCP Server & Integration

Is the MCP server free to use?

Yes! During our Launch Offer, all MCP Tool Calling is completely FREE. This includes:

  • gtm_consultation - GTM consultation summary using the EPIC framework
  • epic_audit - EPIC framework scores
  • generate_roadmap - GTM implementation roadmap

No API key required. Just connect and use!

Which AI assistants can use GTM Alpha?

GTM Alpha works with any MCP-compatible client:

  • Claude (web, desktop and mobile) - Add https://gtmalpha.netlify.app/mcp-sse as a custom connector
  • Cursor / Windsurf - Via mcp.json configuration
  • VS Code - With MCP extension
  • ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot - Via the MCP endpoint (Streamable HTTP)

See our Integration Guide for setup instructions.

Is data from my AI conversations stored?

No. The MCP server receives only the tool name and the inputs of each call, uses them to build the reply, and keeps nothing: our code does not store or log tool inputs or results. Netlify, which hosts the site, keeps its own request logs under its policy. See the privacy policy.
