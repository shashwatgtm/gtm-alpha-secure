Fit and requirements

What do I need to run an EPIC audit?

One required input: a description of your GTM challenge. Add your stage (pre-seed, seed, series A, series B, series C or bootstrapped) and industry. For a full score rather than a preliminary one, also give your average contract value in US dollars, deal cycle in days, net revenue retention in percent, number of target accounts, whether customers can sign up without sales, your main deal source (referrals, outbound, partnerships, inbound or mixed) and your primary market (India, US and EU, Middle East, APAC or global).

  1. Fill in the free EPIC audit form, or add https://gtmalpha.gtmhelix.com/mcp to Claude as a custom connector with no sign-in and ask for an EPIC audit.
  2. Give those details.
  3. Read the four motion scores from 1 to 10, the motion to lead with, and any warnings.
Who is GTM Alpha not ideal for?

Teams that want an AI-written strategy: the EPIC scores follow fixed rules from the documented rubric, and no AI model writes them. Companies that cannot share basic numbers: without contract value, deal cycle, net revenue retention and target account count, the result is marked preliminary. Anyone who needs live market data: GTM Alpha looks nothing up and works only from what you enter.

The EPIC framework and the free audit

What is the EPIC framework?

EPIC is Shashwat Ghosh's go-to-market framework. It names four motions:

  • E: Ecosystem and ABM (account-based marketing and partners)
  • P: Product-Led Growth
  • I: Inbound and Outbound demand generation
  • C: Community-Led growth and advocacy

GTM Alpha scores each one from 1 to 10 for your company and names the one to lead with.

What is in the free EPIC audit?
  • Your EPIC scores for the four motions from 1 to 10, with the rule behind each
  • Recommendations for your lead motion
  • A 180 day plan in four stages: days 1 to 30, days 31 to 60, the first quarter (90 days) and the second quarter (180 days)
  • A PDF download of the report

It is free while GTM Alpha is in testing, and no payment is taken.

Using it in Claude

Is it free to use in Claude?

Yes. The three GTM Alpha tools are free, with no account, sign-in or key:

  • GTM Consultation: EPIC scores and the motion to lead with
  • EPIC Audit: EPIC scores with the rules behind them
  • GTM Roadmap: a 30, 60 or 90 day plan for one motion
Which AI assistants can use GTM Alpha?
  • Claude (web, desktop and mobile): add https://gtmalpha.gtmhelix.com/mcp as a custom connector
  • Cursor, Windsurf and VS Code: add the same address in their connector settings
  • ChatGPT, Gemini and Copilot: through the same address

The Use it in Claude page has the steps for each.

Is data from my AI conversations stored?

No. GTM Alpha receives only the tool name and the inputs of each call, uses them to build the reply, and keeps nothing: our code does not store or log tool inputs or results. Netlify, which hosts the site, keeps its own request logs under its policy. See the privacy policy.

Data and privacy

What data does GTM Alpha collect?

Only what you type in, and most of it is not kept:

  • Free EPIC audit form: your name and role, company name, description, website, LinkedIn URL, challenge, stage, team size and budget, plus the optional EPIC inputs you choose to give. They build your report in that request and are not stored (the form does not ask for email)
  • EPIC audit API for developers: the inputs sent are stored with the result
  • Usage data: none collected by our pages; Netlify, our host, keeps standard request logs
  • GTM Alpha in Claude: nothing is stored or logged by our code

We do not collect passwords, payment details or tracking cookies.

Is my business data kept confidential?

Yes. The free EPIC audit form stores nothing, and the records the EPIC audit API stores cannot be read back through any public address. We:

  • Never share your data with competitors
  • Do not use your data to train AI models
  • Use one hosting provider: Netlify. The report page also loads its PDF library from cdnjs.cloudflare.com (see the privacy page)
How can I access, correct, or delete my data?

Email shashwat@gtmhelix.com with one of these subjects:

  • "Data Access Request": to get a copy of your data
  • "Data Correction Request": to fix inaccurate information
  • "Data Deletion Request": to have your data erased

We respond within 30 days.

What do you do about GDPR, DPDP and CCPA?

We describe what we do rather than claim compliance:

  • The privacy policy lists every field the site stores and where it is kept
  • The free EPIC audit form and GTM Alpha in Claude store and log nothing
  • Our pages set no cookies and load no analytics
  • You can ask for a copy, a correction or deletion by email, and we act on it by hand within 30 days

See our Privacy Policy for complete details.

Do you use cookies or tracking?

No. Our pages set no cookies and include no analytics or tracking scripts:

  • No third-party trackers: no Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel or ad tracking
  • Fonts: served from this site. The one outside request is on the report page, which loads its PDF library from cdnjs.cloudflare.com
