Fit and requirements

In Claude, only your GTM challenge is required. The web form also asks for your name, role, company, industry, a short company description, stage, team size and budget. Add your stage (pre-seed, seed, series A, series B, series C or bootstrapped) and industry. For a full score rather than a preliminary one, also give your average contract value in US dollars, deal cycle in days, net revenue retention in percent, number of target accounts, whether customers can sign up without sales, your main deal source (referrals, outbound, partnerships, inbound or mixed) and your primary market (India, US and EU, Middle East, APAC or global).

  1. Fill in the free EPIC audit form, or add https://gtmalpha.gtmhelix.com/mcp to Claude as a custom connector with no sign-in and ask for an EPIC audit.
  2. Give those details.
  3. Read the four motion scores from 1 to 10, the motion to lead with, and any warnings.

Teams that want an AI-written strategy: the EPIC scores follow fixed rules from the documented rubric, and no AI model writes them. Companies that cannot share basic numbers: without contract value, deal cycle, net revenue retention and target account count, the result is marked preliminary. Anyone who needs live market data: GTM Alpha looks nothing up and works only from what you enter.

The EPIC framework and the free audit

EPIC is Shashwat Ghosh's go-to-market framework. It names four motions:

  • E: Ecosystem and ABM (account-based marketing and partners)
  • P: Product-Led Growth
  • I: Inbound and Outbound demand generation
  • C: Community-Led growth and advocacy

GTM Alpha scores each one from 1 to 10 for your company and names the one to lead with.

  • Your EPIC scores for the four motions from 1 to 10, with the rule behind each
  • Recommendations for your lead motion
  • A 180 day plan in four stages: days 1 to 30, days 31 to 60, the first quarter (90 days) and the second quarter (180 days)
  • A PDF download of the report

It is free while GTM Alpha is in testing, and no payment is taken.
