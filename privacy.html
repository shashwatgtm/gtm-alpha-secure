Privacy policy

For founders, CEOs, CMOs, CROs, demand generation heads and security reviewers. The decision: whether you can enter real company metrics into GTM Alpha safely.

GTM Alpha is stateless: your audit answers and MCP tool inputs are processed in memory only to return your report and are never stored, logged, or used for AI training. By Shashwat Ghosh, Co-Founder and Fractional CMO, Helix GTM Consulting · Last updated 5 November 2026

Built by Shashwat Ghosh, AI native fractional CMO, Co-Founder of Helix GTM Consulting, with 24+ years in B2B and 10+ years of fractional experience.

About Shashwat
In short
The free EPIC audit form, the EPIC audit API for developers and GTM Alpha in Claude keep nothing you type. What stays on our servers: New audits are not saved. Any records from earlier versions are purged automatically after 90 days, or within 7 days on request to shashwat@gtmhelix.com. Our pages count visits with Google Analytics, which sets cookies only if you accept, and Ahrefs Web Analytics. This page explains what we collect, why, how long we keep it and what you can ask us to do.

2. What we collect

2.1 The free EPIC audit form

What you enterWhyDo we keep it?
Your name and role, company name, company description, GTM challenge, industry, business stage, team size, monthly budget, primary focus, company website and LinkedIn address, and any of the optional questions you answer (contract value, deal cycle, net revenue retention, addressable accounts, main deal source, primary market and self serve sign up)To build your free EPIC audit reportNo. Your answers go to our server, which checks them, builds your report straight away and sends it back to your browser. The form does not ask for your email address and takes no payment, so it asks for no payment details. Your report stays in your browser tab. If you download it as a PDF, the file is saved on your device.

2.2 The EPIC audit API for developers

Developers can send company details to the EPIC audit API (described in the API docs). What stays on our servers: New audits are not saved. Any records from earlier versions are purged automatically after 90 days, or within 7 days on request to shashwat@gtmhelix.com.

2.3 GTM Alpha in Claude and other apps

When your AI assistant uses a GTM Alpha tool, our server receives the name of the tool and the details for that request, and uses them only to build the answer. Our code does not save or log the details or the answer, and it never sees the rest of your conversation. It does count the call (see 2.5). Answers are marked so that they are not stored in any cache.

2.4 What is collected automatically

  • Our pages count visits with Google Analytics and Ahrefs Web Analytics. Google Analytics sets cookies only if you select Accept in the cookie choice, and Ahrefs sets none. Neither receives what you type. The usage count in 2.5 is separate.
  • Netlify, which hosts this site, handles each visit (including your IP address and browser details) to deliver the page and to apply the rate limits, and keeps its own logs: netlify.com/privacy.
  • Our pages load their fonts, images and scripts from this site.

2.5 Usage counts

Since 17 October 2026, each audit from the free EPIC audit form or the EPIC audit API, and each GTM Alpha tool call, adds one record to a usage count, kept in Netlify's storage for this site. It shows us which tools people use. Each record holds only:

  • The date and time of the call.
  • This site's name and the tool's name.
  • One word for the kind of app that made the call, such as claude, chatgpt or browser. It is read from the User-Agent header the app sends.
  • Whether the call came from the website or an AI assistant.

It never holds your answers, your inputs, the report, your IP address or the full User-Agent. Nothing in a record says who you are.

2.6 Older records

Earlier versions of this site saved the answers people gave in an older consultation form, in the same database at Netlify. For orders placed when that form took payments, the record also holds the service chosen and its price. These records hold no card or bank details. Nothing adds to them now, and any records from earlier versions are purged automatically after 90 days, or within 7 days on request to shashwat@gtmhelix.com.

3. How we use your data

  • To build your free EPIC audit report and EPIC scores from your answers
  • To answer requests to the EPIC audit API and the GTM Alpha tools
  • To meet our legal obligations

We do not sell your data, share it with competitors or use it to train AI models.

4. Who else sees your data

  • Netlify: hosts the site, stores the usage counts, holds the database (older EPIC audit API records and the older consultation records) and keeps request logs, on our behalf
  • Legal authorities: only when the law requires it

6. Your rights

Under GDPR (EU), DPDP (India), CCPA (California) and similar laws, you have the right to:
  • Access: ask for a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: have wrong data corrected
  • Deletion: have your data deleted
  • Portability: receive your data in a file a computer can read
  • Restriction: limit how we use your data
  • Objection: object to uses based on our legitimate interests
  • Withdrawing consent: where we rely on your consent

To use these rights, email shashwat@gtmhelix.com with the subject "Data Rights Request". Each saved record carries the company name you gave and the time it was saved, so please include your company name and roughly when you used the site. Any records from earlier versions are purged automatically after 90 days, or within 7 days on request to shashwat@gtmhelix.com.

7. Cookies

Our pages set no advertising cookies or tracking pixels. Google Analytics sets analytics cookies only if you select Accept.

8. Where your data is processed

  • India: where we are based
  • United States: where Netlify hosts the site

Netlify processes this data in the United States under its own data processing terms.

9. Security

  • Every page and request is encrypted (HTTPS)
  • Every page is sent with security settings that, among other things, stop other websites from showing this site inside theirs
  • We hold no user accounts or passwords, and take no payments
  • Rate limits: the free EPIC audit form, the EPIC audit API and the status check share 30 requests a minute per visitor, and the GTM Alpha tools allow 300 a minute
  • The free EPIC audit form accepts only its own questions, each with a length limit, and a hidden field turns away automated submissions
  • Size limits: a request to the free EPIC audit form or the EPIC audit API may hold at most 32,000 bytes, with at most 4,000 characters for the company description and for the GTM challenge; a request to the GTM Alpha tools may hold at most 65,536 bytes, with at most 4,000 characters in each text input
  • Saved records cannot be read back through any public address

10. Children

GTM Alpha is not meant for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
