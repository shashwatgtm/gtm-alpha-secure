Privacy policy

By Shashwat Ghosh, Co-Founder and Fractional CMO, Helix GTM Consulting · Last updated 29 September 2026

Built by Shashwat Ghosh, Co-Founder and Fractional CMO, Helix GTM Consulting, with 24+ years in B2B and 10+ years fractional.

About Shashwat
In short
The free EPIC audit form, the EPIC audit API for developers and GTM Alpha in Claude keep nothing you type. New audits are not kept, and older records are deleted on request within 30 days. Our pages set no cookies and run no analytics. This page explains what we collect, why, how long we keep it and what you can ask us to do.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Helix GTM Consulting ("we", "us", "our") decides how the personal data collected through this website and the GTM Alpha tools is used.

2. What we collect

2.1 The free EPIC audit form

What you enterWhyDo we keep it?
Your name and role, company name, company description, GTM challenge, industry, business stage, team size, monthly budget, primary focus, company website and LinkedIn address, and any of the optional questions you answer (contract value, deal cycle, net revenue retention, addressable accounts, main deal source, primary market and self-serve sign-up)To build your free EPIC audit reportNo. Your answers go to our server, which checks them, builds your report straight away and sends it back to your browser. Our code does not save or log your answers or your report, and sends them to no one

The form does not ask for your email address and takes no payment, so it asks for no payment details. Your report stays in your browser tab. If you download it as a PDF, the file is saved on your device.

2.2 The EPIC audit API for developers

Developers can send company details to the EPIC audit API (described in the API docs). New audits are not kept: what they send is used only to build the result, and our code does not save it. Audits that earlier versions of the API saved stay in a database at Netlify, the company that hosts this site, and are deleted on request within 30 days.

2.3 GTM Alpha in Claude and other AI assistants

When your AI assistant uses a GTM Alpha tool, our server receives the name of the tool and the details for that request, and uses them only to build the answer. Our code does not save or log them, and it never sees the rest of your conversation. Answers are marked so that they are not stored in any cache.

2.4 What is collected automatically

  • Our pages set no cookies and run no analytics or tracking.
  • Netlify, which hosts this site, handles each visit (including your IP address and browser details) to deliver the page and to apply the rate limits, and keeps its own logs: netlify.com/privacy.
  • Our pages load their fonts, images and scripts from this site.

2.5 Older records

Earlier versions of this site saved the answers people gave in an older consultation form, in the same database at Netlify. For orders placed when that form took payments, the record also holds the service chosen and its price. These records hold no card or bank details. Nothing adds to them now, and they are deleted on request within 30 days.

3. How we use your data

  • To build your free EPIC audit report and EPIC scores from your answers
  • To answer requests to the EPIC audit API and the GTM Alpha tools
  • To meet our legal obligations

We do not sell your data, share it with competitors or use it to train AI models.

4. Who else sees your data

  • Netlify: hosts the site, holds the database (older EPIC audit API records and the older consultation records) and keeps request logs, on our behalf
  • Legal authorities: only when the law requires it

6. Your rights

Under GDPR (EU), DPDP (India), CCPA (California) and similar laws, you have the right to:
  • Access: ask for a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: have wrong data corrected
  • Deletion: have your data deleted
  • Portability: receive your data in a file a computer can read
  • Restriction: limit how we use your data
  • Objection: object to uses based on our legitimate interests
  • Withdrawing consent: where we rely on your consent

To use these rights, email shashwat@gtmhelix.com with the subject "Data Rights Request". Each saved record carries the company name you gave and the time it was saved, so please include your company name and roughly when you used the site. We find and delete records by hand within 30 days.

7. Cookies

Our pages set no cookies and include no analytics, advertising cookies or tracking pixels.

8. Where your data is processed

  • India: where we are based
  • United States: where Netlify hosts the site

Netlify processes this data in the United States under its own data processing terms.

9. Security

  • Every page and request is encrypted (HTTPS)
  • Every page is sent with security settings that, among other things, stop other websites from showing this site inside theirs
  • We hold no user accounts or passwords, and take no payments
  • Rate limits: the free EPIC audit form, the EPIC audit API and the status check share 30 requests a minute per visitor, and the GTM Alpha tools allow 300 a minute
  • The free EPIC audit form accepts only its own questions, each with a length limit, and a hidden field turns away automated submissions
  • Size limits: a request to the free EPIC audit form or the EPIC audit API may hold at most 32,000 bytes, with at most 4,000 characters for the company description and for the GTM challenge; a request to the GTM Alpha tools may hold at most 65,536 bytes, with at most 4,000 characters in each text input
  • Saved records cannot be read back through any public address

10. Children

GTM Alpha is not meant for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Changes are published on this page with a new date.

12. Complaints

If you have concerns about how we handle data:

  2. You can also complain to your local data protection authority
