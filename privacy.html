2.2 EPIC audit API

If you or an application send data to /api/epic-audit, the inputs are saved with the audit result in our Netlify Blobs database (store "epic-audits").

2.3 MCP server

When an AI assistant calls a GTM Alpha tool through /mcp-sse, the server receives the tool name and the inputs for that call and uses them only to build the reply. Our code does not store or log these inputs or the results, and it never receives your conversation.

2.4 Data collected automatically

  • Our pages include no analytics or tracking scripts and set no cookies.
  • Netlify, which hosts this site, processes each request (including your IP address and browser details) to deliver it and keeps its own logs: netlify.com/privacy.
  • Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, the home page loads one image from gamma.app, and the generated report loads a PDF library from cdnjs. These providers receive your IP address when your browser loads those files.

2.5 Older records

Earlier versions of this site also saved consultation records in two other stores ("gtm-consultations" and "consultations"). The endpoints that wrote to them no longer accept new data. Any existing records are kept until you ask us to delete them.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Provide GTM consultation and analysis services
  • Generate EPIC framework reports and roadmaps
  • Process payments through PayPal
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your data, share it with competitors or use it to train AI models.

4. Data Sharing

  • Netlify: hosting, our Blobs database and request logs (as a data processor)

6. Your Rights

Under GDPR (EU), DPDP (India), CCPA (California), and similar laws, you have the right to:
  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Rectification: Correct inaccurate data
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Portability: Receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Restriction: Limit how we process your data
  • Object: Object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Withdraw Consent: Where processing is based on consent

To exercise these rights, email us at shashwat@hyperplays.in with subject "Data Rights Request". Records are stored by company name and time, so please include your company name and roughly when you used the site. We find and delete records by hand within 30 days.

7. Cookies

Our pages set no cookies and include no analytics, advertising cookies or tracking pixels. PayPal sets its own cookies on its checkout pages.

8. International Transfers

Your data may be processed in:

  • India: Primary business location
  • United States: Netlify hosting, PayPal processing

Transfers are protected by Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) where required.

9. Security

  • HTTPS encryption for all data in transit
  • Payments handled on PayPal's pages; we never see card or bank details
  • No user accounts or passwords are held by us
  • Rate limits on the forms, the APIs and the MCP server
  • Stored records cannot be read back through any public endpoint

10. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
